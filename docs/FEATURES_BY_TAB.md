## Getting started

**Sign up / log in**
- **Create free account**: enter your name and business, then the code we send you **on WhatsApp**.
- **Log in**: enter your phone number, then the WhatsApp code (tap **Resend** if it doesn't arrive).
- If you manage more than one business, you'll be asked **which workspace to open**.

**First-time setup (Pro)**
1. Find your business: search by name or paste your Google Maps link.
2. Confirm name, address, phone and website.
3. **Connect Google Profile**: sign in with the Google account that manages your listing.
4. Pick your listing if you have more than one.
5. **Train your AI**: choose a tone of voice (Professional, Friendly…) and add anything the AI should know.

**Switching business**: use **Active Workspace** at the top of the website sidebar to switch between your businesses.

# Website

Left sidebar: **Dashboard · Audit Engine · SEO Plan · Profile optimization · Google Profile · Review Management · CRM · Content · Settings · Billing · Profile**

### 1. Dashboard
- **Performance Overview**: profile views, calls, direction requests and website clicks.
- **CRM & AI Activity**: new leads and what the AI did for you recently.
- **Insights** (opened from the dashboard): Search views, Maps views, website clicks, calls and directions, this month vs last month. Includes the keywords people use to find you, with monthly search counts. Tap **Sync Now** to refresh.

### 2. Audit Engine
- A full health check of your Google Business Profile, with a **score**.
- Compares you with **competitors nearby** and checks your SEO signals.
- Gives you a **fix list** in priority order.
- Keeps every past audit so you can see your progress over time.

### 3. SEO Plan
Your step-by-step plan to rank higher on Google Maps:
- **Key Finding**: the single biggest thing to fix.
- **Target keywords**: the searches you should show up for, with real Google search volume, your current rank, and who ranks above you (competitors).
- **Action plan**: tasks in order, with a % complete.
- **Ready-made drafts**: business title and description, suggested services, extra Google categories, and suggested Q&As.
- **Weekly post plan** and **Your USP**, which are used in your posts and review replies.
- **Baseline history**: rating, review count and average rank over time.

### 4. Profile optimization
Improve your live Google profile one section at a time: **description, categories, services, opening hours (including holidays), service area, booking/order links, attributes**.
- Each section shows your current Google value next to our suggestion.
- Edit if you like, then tap **Yes, apply to Google**. Nothing is sent without your OK.
- Track changes that show **Google is reviewing**, and tap **Check Google again** to refresh.
- **Undo / roll back** any change you applied.

### 5. Google Profile
- See and edit your live listing: name, address, phone, website, category and description.

### 6. Review Management
**Monitor Reviews tab**
- All your Google reviews in one place, with filters: Positive, Negative, Critical, Unanswered.
- **AI-drafted reply** for every review. Edit it and post it to Google.
- Stats: average rating, response rate, sentiment score, pending reviews.
- **Reply mode**: *AI drafts, I approve*, or *Auto-reply to all reviews*. You also choose the reply tone.

**Review Requests tab**
- Ask customers for a Google review **on WhatsApp**. Add them one by one, import them from the CRM, or send a **Quick review request**.
- Track every request: Pending → Delivered → Read → Clicked → Completed.
- An automatic follow-up goes to customers who haven't left a review yet.
- **Google review QR code** you can print for your counter or reception.

### 7. CRM (AI Lead Manager) — *included in the Free plan*
- **List, Kanban** (drag leads between stages) and **Analytics** views.
- **Add lead**: name, phone, deal value, notes.
- **Import leads** from a CSV or Excel file. Duplicates are skipped automatically.
- **Lead details**: timeline, notes, calls, meetings, follow-up tasks and appointments.
- Move leads through stages: Active → Converted / Closed.
- **Growth report**: the month's leads, conversions and follow-ups on one page.

### 8. Content (AI Content Studio)
- **Autopilot**: a new Google post is written for you every week.
- **This week's offer**: type your offer and a promotional post is made from it.
- **Create Post**: pick a tone, then review the AI's title, text, hashtags and button. **Publish now** or schedule it for a date and time.
- **Existing posts**: edit or delete posts that are already live on Google.
- **FAQ** tab (questions & answers for your profile), **SEO** tab (optimized description + SEO score), and **History**.

### 9. Settings
- **Business details**: name, category, description, keywords.
- **Google Business Profile**: connect or disconnect.
- **Notifications**: choose what you hear about, and whether you get it on WhatsApp. Options: new leads, new reviews, critical 1–2★ reviews, weekly summary, report ready, low content buffer, and a Google performance update every 15 days.

### 10. Billing
- Current plan, renewal/trial date, this month's usage (resets on the 1st), plan limits and invoices.
- **Upgrade Plan**: choose monthly or yearly and pay at checkout.

### 11. Profile
- Edit your name and company, change your password, or delete your account.

# Mobile App

Bottom tabs: **Home · Performance · Posts · Reviews · CRM**. Everything else is under **More**.

### Home
- A quick view of how your business is doing, plus tips for **better ranking, more reviews and more customers**.
- Shortcuts such as **Add Fresh Photos**.

### Performance
- Views, calls, website clicks and directions from Google, shown over time.

### Posts
- **Posts**: tap **+**, write your post, add an image, check the **Preview**, then **Publish now** or **Schedule post**.
- Edit or delete posts that are already live on Google.
- **Photos**: see all your Google profile photos and upload new ones. They publish right after upload.

### Reviews
- **Overview**: rating breakdown and review trend for the last 8 weeks.
- **Reviews**: filter by rating, *Needs reply* or *Replied*.
- Tap a review → **Generate suggestion** → edit → **Approve reply** → **Post reply to Google**. You can also **Regenerate** or **Reject** the suggestion.
- **Review Requests**: see the WhatsApp review requests you've sent.

### CRM — *included in the Free plan*
- Search leads by name, phone or email.
- **Add lead**: phone (or paste a number), name, value in ₹, and how the call went.
- **Scan visiting card**: take a photo or pick one from your gallery. The details are filled in for you and a lead is created. You can then **Send on WhatsApp**, **Send email**, or **Save to phone contacts**.
- **From contacts**: import leads from your phone's contacts.
- **Recent calls**: turn any call into a lead (**Save as Lead** or **Save + call-back task**), link it to an existing lead, or dismiss it.
- **Lead details**: stage, notes, history, and a shortcut to the chat inbox.
- **Monthly Growth**: leads received, conversion, completed and overdue follow-ups.

### More → Account
| Menu item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Notifications** | All alerts in one list: new reviews, new leads, reports ready |
| **Settings** | Business details, WhatsApp number, keywords (up to 20), brand for post images, notification preferences, disconnect Google |
| **Review settings** | Reply mode (approve first / auto-reply) and reply tone |
| **Billing** | Plan, this month's usage, cancel subscription, *Questions? Chat with us* |
| **Profile** | Edit name and company, change password, log out |

## Quick help

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| The WhatsApp code didn't arrive | Check the number and country code, wait 30 seconds, then tap **Resend**. |
| "Connect Google Business Profile first" | Go to **Settings → Google Business Profile** and connect the Google account that owns your listing. |
| My listing isn't in the list | You're signed in with a different Google account. Disconnect, then connect with the right one. |
| A change says "Google is reviewing" | This is normal. Google can take from a few minutes to a few days. Tap **Check Google again**. |
| A tab is locked 🔒 | That feature is part of Pro. Go to **Billing → Upgrade Plan**. |
| "Limit reached" | You've used this month's allowance. It resets on the 1st, or you can upgrade. |
| The app and website show different data | Pull down to refresh in the app, or reload the website. |
